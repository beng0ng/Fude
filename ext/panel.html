BYour own music

Compose it, or hand Fude the sheet music

Fude plays it with the instruments you choose, one part at a time or all together, and paints as it goes.

Each part gets its own instrument. With one part, the arrangement writes the other hands from your melody; with several, it only picks instruments.
Note names with an optional octave and length in beats, like C, F#5 or Bb4:2. Use r for a rest.

Put Fude on your home screen

  1. Tap the Share button ⎙ in Safari’s toolbar.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Fude from its icon. It runs full screen, works offline, and keeps your colors and paintings.

Fude

Fude only listens while you ask it to, and it works out the notes on this device. Nothing it hears is recorded or sent anywhere.

Your colors, paintings and settings stay on this device too. If you link Spotify, Fude reads which song is playing, and you can unlink it at any time from the ⋯ menu.
