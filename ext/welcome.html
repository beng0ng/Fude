Fude

Welcome to Fude

Fude turns the music playing in your browser into paintings, right in the side panel. Here is how to start.

  1. Pin Fude to the toolbar. Click the puzzle piece at the right of the address bar, then the pin next to Fude.
  2. Play some music in a tab. Spotify’s web player, YouTube, YouTube Music, Apple Music, SoundCloud, Deezer, Tidal, Bandcamp or any other site.
  3. Click the Fude button on that tab, or press Alt + Shift + F (Option + Shift + F on a Mac). The side panel opens and starts listening to the tab. Headphones are fine; no microphone is needed.
  4. Pick a picture and its colors. A little cat paints it from the bottom up while the song plays. You’ll see the whole painting when the song ends, and it’s saved to your gallery.

Good to know

  • Music in another tab: choose Switch tab in the ⋯ menu, or click the Fude button on that tab.
  • Desktop apps like the Spotify app: turn on Hear the whole computer in the ⋯ menu, choose Entire screen and turn on Share system audio. This works on Windows and ChromeOS; on a Mac, play the music in a tab instead.
  • When the music stops, the cat stops and waits. After a short while the painting finishes, unless you press Pause.
  • Pets and effects unlock the more you listen and paint. Find them in the ⋯ menu, next to Your gallery.
  • If Fude says it can’t hear the tab, click the Fude button again while the music tab is in front. Chrome only lets an extension hear a tab after you click its button there.

Private by design

The sound is turned into notes on your computer and is never recorded or sent anywhere. Fude has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
