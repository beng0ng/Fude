Privacy

Muse · updated 29 October 2026

Muse does not collect anything about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no server of its own. What it hears and what you make stay on your device.

The microphone

Muse uses the microphone only while you have asked it to listen, for example after you press Start. It works out the notes being played in real time, on your device, and uses them to paint. The sound is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Your phone shows that the microphone is in use whenever Muse is listening, and you can turn the permission off in your phone’s settings at any time.

In the browser extension, Muse can listen to a browser tab that you choose instead of the microphone. The same applies: the sound is turned into notes on your computer and is never kept or sent.

What stays on your device

Your colors, your paintings and gallery, your own music and your settings (such as light or dark) are kept in Muse’s storage on your device. They are not uploaded. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes them.

Spotify, if you link it

Linking Spotify is optional. You sign in on Spotify’s own page, and Spotify gives Muse a key that is kept on your device. Muse uses it to ask Spotify which song is playing, how far through it is and its cover art, and to skip, pause or play when you press those buttons in Muse. These requests go straight from your device to Spotify. Spotify’s own privacy policy covers what Spotify does with them. You can unlink Spotify at any time from the ⋯ menu, which deletes the key from your device.

Things that use the internet

  • Searching for a painting online. The words you type are sent to Wikimedia Commons, The Metropolitan Museum of Art and the Art Institute of Chicago to find public-domain paintings, and the pictures load from them.
  • Song links. When you paste a link from a music app, Muse shows that service’s own player (such as Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, SoundCloud, Deezer or Tidal), which loads from that service.
  • Sharing. When you share a painting, Muse makes an image or a link that holds its colors and notes. It goes only where you choose to send it.

Those services have their own privacy policies. Muse does not send them anything about you beyond what each request needs.

Children

Muse collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
